OpenMap v0.4.0-beta supplied terrain/cave references used to generate route hints. Its MIT code notice is retained in OPENMAP-LICENSE.txt. Blizzard map-art ownership is unchanged; OpenMap map images and the standalone OpenMap addon are not included here.

ElevationData.lua contains samples extracted from a local WoW client and is excluded. Restore it from your matching local addon with tools/build_local.py. Other authored numeric terrain preferences and routing code retain their existing behavior.
